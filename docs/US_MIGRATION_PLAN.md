# Plan: moving InsureX Nexus AI from India to the US market

Status: Phase 0 done (2026-11-23). Phases 1–9 not started.

## Goal

Make InsureX a US personal-auto product for US carriers. Rating, coverages, claims, fraud signals, models, demo data and the UI should all use US rules and dollars. The synthetic **Lumora Mutual** dataset (generated by `motorsynth`) becomes the training and benchmark data.

This plan covers personal auto (Wave 1). Homeowners, renters and condo (Wave 2) and personal umbrella (Wave 3) follow it, as set out in [US_PRODUCT_CATALOG.md](US_PRODUCT_CATALOG.md). Phase 2 builds the policy model so those lines can be added without another rebuild.

## Starting point

The codebase began as US-shaped, and India-specific logic was added on top. Several US pieces are already in place:

| Already US-ready | India-specific (to replace) |
| --- | --- |
| `Vehicle.vin`, `license_state`, `purchase_price` | `Vehicle.engine_cc`, `ex_showroom_price`, `registration_city` |
| `PolicyCoverage`: liability, collision, comprehensive, UM, MedPay, PIP, rental, glass | `apps/policies/services/rating.py` + `POLICY_RATING_TABLES`: IRDAI third-party premium by cc, IDV, NCB, 18% GST, Zone A/B cities |
| `Customer.license_state`, `credit_score` | `apps/policies/plans.py` add-ons (zero depreciation, engine protect, CPA) |
| `Claim.police_report`, `number_of_vehicles`, `injuries` | `apps/assessments/repair_estimate.py`: Indian labour and parts rates, IRDAI depreciation |
| `backend/data/insurance_claims.csv`, `fraud_oracle.csv` (original US-dollar files) | `backend/data/india/` generator; models trained in rupees (`claim_amount_inr`, `district` in `vehicle_fraud.py`) |
| | `apps/assessments/explain.py` reason texts (FIR, IDV) |
| | Customer sign-in by Indian plate plus 10-digit mobile (`apps/accounts/views.py`) |
| | `load_demo` (Bengaluru, Gurugram, Chennai, `KA 01 MJ 4521`, `+91`) |
| | Frontend: `formatCurrency` defaults to `INR`/`en-IN` (`frontend/src/utils/helpers.js`); `PolicyForm`, `RequestPolicy`, `VehicleForm`, `PlanCompare`, `ClaimDetails` |

About 45 source files mention India-specific terms. The five heaviest are `rating.py`, `load_demo.py`, `repair_estimate.py`, `explain.py` and `PolicyForm.jsx`.

## Lumora → InsureX mapping

| Lumora table | InsureX target | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `customers` | `accounts.User` (role `customer`) + `customers.Customer` | Phones are 555 numbers and emails use `.example` domains, so it's safe to demo |
| `drivers`, `policy_drivers` | **new** `PolicyDriver` (or `customers.Driver`) | Needed for unlisted-driver and young-driver signals |
| `vehicles` | `vehicles.Vehicle` | `body_type`→`body_style`, `msrp`→new `msrp`, add `garaging_zip`, `declared_annual_miles`, `declared_usage`, `adas_equipped`, `lienholder_flag` |
| `policy_terms` + `policy_vehicles` | `policies.Policy` + `PolicyCoverage` | **Structural gap:** a Lumora term covers several vehicles, while `Policy.vehicle` is one FK. Phase 2 imports one `Policy` per term × vehicle; multi-vehicle policies come later (Phase 6) |
| `producers` | **new** `Producer` (agency/agent) linked from `Policy` | Needed for garaging-evasion and ghost-policy signals |
| `employees` | staff `User`s (employee, investigator) + workforce profiles | `authority_limit` is new |
| `claims` | `claims.Claim` | `loss_cause`→`incident_type` mapping; add `reported_at`, `loss_state/zip`, `odometer_at_loss`, `catastrophe_code`, `fault_indicator`, `attorney_represented`, paid/reserve/incurred totals |
| `claim_features` | **new** `ClaimFeature` (coverage × claimant) | Core US claims concept: reserves, payments, subrogation and salvage per coverage |
| `claim_persons` | **new** `ClaimParty` | Third parties and passengers; needed for ring detection |
| `estimate_lines` | **new** `EstimateLine` | Padding and supplement signals |
| `medical_bills` | **new** `MedicalBill` | PIP-mill and injury-buildup signals |
| `payments`, `recoveries` | `settlements` (extend) or **new** `ClaimPayment`, `Recovery` | Duplicate-payment, shell-vendor and leakage signals |
| `service_providers` | **new** `providers` app (shops, clinics, tow, attorneys, glass) | `repair_shops` is retired; don't reuse it |
| `siu_investigations` | `investigations.Investigation` + `investigation_outcome` | This is the **only** label imported into the app |
| `ground_truth/*` | **never imported into the app database** | Used offline for training and benchmarking only, so no truth leaks into the product |

## Phases

Sizes are rough: S is about 1–2 days, M is about a week, L is two weeks or more.

### Phase 0: Decisions and groundwork (S) — done 2026-11-23
- Answer the [open questions](#open-questions-to-settle-before-phase-1) below.
- Vendor the `motorsynth` generator into `backend/data/us/motorsynth/`. Commit `config.yaml`, not the generated CSVs, the same way India data is handled now.
- Add a Jenkins stage that generates a 50K sample with a fixed seed, so CI doesn't need stored datasets.
- **Done when:** decisions are written down here and `python -m motorsynth.generate config.yaml --terms 50000` runs in CI.

### Phase 1: Locale and currency (S) — in progress 2026-11-23
- `formatCurrency` and `formatCurrencyCompact` default to `USD` / `en-US`. Remove every hard-coded `₹`.
- Use US phone (`(555) 555-0123`), ZIP and state formats in forms and validators. Collect a US plate and state.
- Customer sign-in: plate + state + last 4 digits of phone (or email + one-time code) instead of a 10-digit Indian mobile.
- Set `ML_CURRENCY_PER_DATASET_UNIT=1` with USD models (after Phase 3).
- **Done when:** no `INR`, `en-IN`, `₹` or `+91` remains in `frontend/src` or the API responses, and the frontend tests pass.
- **Scope note:** the policy rating and plan screens (`PlanCompare`, `PolicyForm`, the tariff part of `PolicyDetails`) and the vehicle rating fields (engine cc, ex-showroom price) stay in rupees until Phase 5, because they show figures from the IRDAI tables. Showing those numbers with a `$` would be misleading. `load_demo` keeps its Indian data until Phase 9.

### Phase 2: Lumora import and domain models (L)
- Policy structure, state rules and the policy model changes are specified in [US_POLICY_SPEC.md](US_POLICY_SPEC.md). It adds `PolicyVehicle` here, so terms no longer need to be split per vehicle.
- Build `Policy` line-agnostic (`line` + `form`, plus a `Household`), as in [US_PRODUCT_CATALOG.md](US_PRODUCT_CATALOG.md) §4, so home lines can be added later.
- New models: `ClaimFeature`, `ClaimParty`, `EstimateLine`, `MedicalBill`, `ClaimPayment`, `Recovery`, `Producer`, `PolicyDriver`, and the `providers` app. Add the new fields on `Vehicle`, `Policy` and `Claim` listed in the mapping.
- New command `import_lumora --dir <path> [--dry-run]`: idempotent and batched (`bulk_create`), loading only the observable tables.
- Remove the India-only fields (`engine_cc`, `ex_showroom_price`, IDV) with migrations, once nothing reads them.
- **Done when:** the 50K sample imports in a few minutes with row counts matching the CSVs, a second run creates nothing new, and `manage.py test` passes.

### Phase 3: Retrain the models on US data and measure them (M)
- New feature builders for Lumora: claim level (timing, report delay, police report, odometer versus declared miles, early attorney, operator not listed, days from bind to loss, prior claims) and feature level (repair-cost and total-loss models per coverage).
- Train the fraud model **two ways**: on `siu_investigations` outcomes (what a real carrier would have) and on `ground_truth` (the upper bound). Report both.
- New command `benchmark_triage`: precision, recall and dollars found at a fixed SIU capacity (1.6% of claims), compared with Lumora's red-flag rules (50% recall, 21% precision).
- Replace `claim_amount_inr`/`district` in `vehicle_fraud.py` with `claim_amount_usd`/`loss_state` + ZIP3.
- **Done when:** `train_project_models` defaults to US data, and `benchmark_triage` writes a report to `backend/ml/models/benchmark_report.json`.

### Phase 4: Triage heuristic v2, with US red flags (M)
- `triage_heuristic_v2.json`: keep the general flags (new policy, late report, repeat vehicle). Replace the FIR flags with police-report flags, and add US flags: loss within 10 days of bind, attorney within N days, odometer far above declared miles, operator not on policy, tow/storage over market rate, glass claim reported by vendor, ADAS calibration on a non-ADAS car, supplement count, and loss state ≠ garaging state.
- Tune the weights against the Phase 3 benchmark, and record the version on each assessment as today.
- Update the reason texts in `explain.py` (no FIR or IDV wording).
- **Done when:** v2 beats v1 and the Lumora rules on the benchmark, and benign catastrophe and pileup claims don't flood the alert queue.

### Phase 5: US rating (L)
- The rating formula, state rules and validator rules are in [US_POLICY_SPEC.md](US_POLICY_SPEC.md) §3–§5.
- Replace `POLICY_RATING_TABLES` with a US structure: base rate by state and coverage, territory factor by ZIP3, and driver class (age, years licensed, violations, at-fault accidents). Add credit tier where the state allows it, vehicle symbol (MSRP and segment), increased-limit factors (BI/PD), deductible factors, and multi-car, homeowner and paid-in-full discounts. Use state minimum limits and the states that require PIP.
- Keep every figure marked `PLACEHOLDER` or `SPEC`. These are indicative only, not filed rates.
- Use rule-based state restrictions: no credit-based rating in CA, HI or MA, and limits in other states.
- **Done when:** quotes for a sample driver in 3–4 states land in a realistic range, and `test_rating.py` is rewritten for US rules.

### Phase 6: US claims handling and settlement (M)
- Reserves and payments at the coverage-feature level. Settle with ACV, deductibles, salvage and subrogation in `settlements`.
- Multi-vehicle policies (a `PolicyVehicle` link table) if Phase 2 flattened them.
- Repair estimate based on US labour rates by metro and part types (OEM, aftermarket, used), with no IRDAI depreciation.
- **Done when:** a claim's net incurred matches paid + reserve − recoveries, and the settlement tests pass.

### Phase 7: Ring detection and the investigator view (M)
- Graph features: shared phone, address, bank-account token, provider, attorney or clinic across parties and claims, plus counts of connected claims.
- Add a "linked claims" panel to the investigation screen, showing why claims are linked, never as a verdict.
- **Done when:** the Phase 3 benchmark shows the gain on the ring scenarios (swoop-and-squat, PIP mill, body shop, tow, glass).

### Phase 8: RL routing realism (S–M)
- Feed SIU capacity and referral patterns from Lumora into `backend/rl/` (the environment and dataset).
- **Done when:** the evaluation gate re-runs on the new environment and its report is updated.

### Phase 9: Demo, docs and compliance (S)
- Rewrite `load_demo` with US customers (for example Columbus, Dallas, Miami and Los Angeles), US plates, 555 phones, and claims that reflect the storylines.
- Update the README, `DATASETS.md`, `WORKFLOW.md` and `PROJECT_REFERENCE.md`. Archive `backend/data/india/`; it stays in git history.
- Add a compliance notes page: the NAIC model bulletin on insurers' use of AI, state rules on unfair discrimination and credit-based rating, the FCRA adverse-action notice when credit is used, and GLBA for customer data. The product stays advisory, with a human deciding.

## Risks

- **Licence:** the `motorsynth` package has no licence file. Confirm that you're allowed to use and redistribute it, and the data it produces, in a product for clients.
- **Synthetic data only:** a model trained on Lumora learns the generator's assumptions. Present benchmark numbers as "on synthetic data". Before a client relies on the scores, retrain on their own settled claims and SIU outcomes.
- **Truth leakage:** `ground_truth/` must never reach the app database or API, or the benchmark becomes meaningless.
- **Schema change size:** Phase 2 touches most apps. Land it in small migrations behind tests, and keep the old fields until nothing reads them.
- **Scale:** the full book has about 1M terms and 148K claims. Import with batching and indexes. Keep the 50K sample for CI and demos.
- **The 1M parquet download is damaged** (no end-of-archive marker). Download it again, or regenerate it with the generator.

## Open questions to settle before Phase 1

1. ~~Replace India completely, or keep India as a second market?~~ **Decided 2026-11-23: US only.** India-specific code, data and fields are removed, not kept behind a setting. They remain in git history.
2. ~~Which states to launch with?~~ **Decided 2026-11-23: OH, TX, FL, GA and CA.** Rating tables, minimum limits and PIP rules cover only these five states at first. FL is no-fault with PIP required, and CA rating must not use credit.
3. ~~Customer sign-in method?~~ **Decided 2026-11-23: email + one-time code.** The code is sent with the existing email backend, expires after a short time, and is rate-limited with the existing throttles. It replaces the plate + mobile sign-in in `apps/accounts/views.py` (Phase 1).
4. ~~Store the 50K sample, or generate it?~~ **Decided 2026-11-23: generate it.** The generator lives in `backend/data/us/`, the output is gitignored, and Jenkins regenerates it on each build.
5. ~~Auto only, or other US personal lines too?~~ **Decided 2026-11-24: auto first, then home, renters and condo, then umbrella.** See [US_PRODUCT_CATALOG.md](US_PRODUCT_CATALOG.md). Health, life and commercial lines are out of scope.
