Indian car prices and repair costs

Real ex-showroom prices, authorised-dealer spare-part prices and body-shop labour rates, collected from public listings. Use them to sanity-check claim amounts and repair estimates in demos.

Read before quoting. Car prices are Delhi ex-showroom from CarDekho (Nov 2026). Spare-part prices are dealer list prices from ZigWheels and CarDekho and mostly come from the previous generation of each model, because current-generation parts aren't listed. Since Sept 2025 auto parts carry 18% GST; ZigWheels still adds 28%, so this page uses 18%. Labour figures are ranges from workshop guides, not OEM rate cards. Confirm with a dealer before using any figure for a real settlement.

Worked repair estimates

Parts plus 18% GST, plus the labour range for the same job. The last line shows what the app now estimates for the same damage (labour + a parts share of the showroom price, calibrated to these figures). The major-damage rate also allows for hidden parts such as the radiator and airbags, so it sits above the visible-parts total.
