InsureX demo vehicle and policy data

This bundle contains 12 anonymized example vehicle records and 12 policy records exported from the current local database. Policy/vehicle relationships use the demo IDs in the CSV files. Customer and agent IDs, original record IDs, original VINs and plates, policy numbers, and exact address values are not included. VINs and plates are fictional placeholders; do not use them for real registration or insurance. Premium and coverage values are sample app data.

To import into another InsureX instance, use the app UI/API or adapt the rows to that database?s required customer and relationship IDs. These CSVs are reference/demo data and are not a direct Django fixture.
